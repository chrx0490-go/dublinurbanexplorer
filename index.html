Sights and culture

  • Trinity College and Book of Kells (rating 4.4): Old university with the famous book. Book your ticket in advance.
  • Dublin Castle (rating 4.3): Grand state rooms and a garden in the middle of the city.
  • Chester Beatty Library (rating 4.7): Museum with old books from around the world. Free entry.
  • St. Patrick's Cathedral (rating 4.5): Largest church in the city, built from 1220.
  • EPIC Irish Emigration Museum (rating 4.6): Modern museum about Irish emigrants.
  • Jeanie Johnston (rating 4.7): Tour on the emigrant ship. Right next to EPIC.
  • National Gallery of Ireland (rating 4.7): European and Irish art. Free entry.
  • National Museum of Ireland, Archaeology (rating 4.6): Gold, bog bodies, ancient finds. Free entry.
  • Guinness Storehouse (rating 4.4): Brewery with a view from the Gravity Bar. Book your ticket in advance.

Parks, shopping and streets

  • St. Stephen's Green: The big park in the city centre.
  • Grafton Street: The shopping street with street musicians.
  • Stephen's Green Shopping Centre (rating 4.2): Shopping centre in a beautiful building.
  • George's Street Arcade (rating 4.1): Covered market with small things and food.
  • Capel Street: Street with many shops and restaurants.
  • Smithfield: Area north of the river.
  • Rathmines: Residential area in the south with cafés and shops.

Pubs and nightlife

  • Temple Bar (area): The famous nightlife area. Touristy, but a must.
  • Fidelity Bar (rating 4.7): Bar and restaurant in Smithfield. Later it turns into a club.
  • The Hacienda Bar (rating 4.7): Small bar with character and a pool table.
  • The Bar With No Name (rating 4.3): Cocktail bar like a big speakeasy.
  • The George (rating 3.7): Bar and club, known for drag shows.
  • Daphni (rating 4.7): Cocktails in the Bolands Mills.
  • NoLIta (rating 4.2): Restaurant and bar. Closed on Monday.
  • Laughter Lounge (rating 4.3): Suggestion: comedy club. Shows only on Friday and Saturday.
  • Abbey Theatre (rating 4.7): Suggestion: the national theatre. Check the programme first.

Coast and day trips

  • Howth Cliff Walk (rating 4.9): Hike along the cliffs with sea views.
  • Beshoff Bros, Fish and Chips (rating 4.4): Fish and chips at Howth harbour.
  • Howth Market (rating 4.3): Market at the harbour with food and crafts.
  • Bray to Greystones Cliff Walk: Coastal walk from Bray to Greystones.
  • Johnnie Fox's Pub (rating 4.6): Old pub in the Wicklow Mountains. Good food.
